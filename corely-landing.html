Everything a subscription business needs to get paid on time

One dashboard for members, tiers, dues, payments and WhatsApp. No spreadsheets to chase, no manual reminders.

Members and membership tiers

Create your own tiers and fees, see Active, Due Soon and Overdue at a glance, and renew with +1 Month. An expired member renews from the day they pay, not the old date.

Automatic WhatsApp reminders

Reminders go out at 9:00 AM IST on their own, through the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API from your own business number.

A Pay button inside the message

Members tap one button in WhatsApp and land on a payment page. Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm or a QR code, no typing of amounts.

Two ways to collect

Direct UPI with no gateway fees, or the Cashfree checkout for cards, netbanking and wallets with automatic payment matching.

Receipts and a clean ledger

Paid members get a WhatsApp confirmation. Every payment lands in a searchable ledger by day, week, month or year, with CSV export.

Smart auto-replies

When a member texts PAID, DUE, FEE or HELP, Corely replies with your business name, their fee and a payment link. Never more than once in 24 hours.

Campaign Studio

Build WhatsApp templates, see Meta approval status live, and send offers or notices to filtered groups of members. No Meta API yet? Use one-tap WhatsApp links.

Live chat console, synced everywhere

Chat with members from inside the app. Changes show instantly on the front desk PC, the manager tablet and the owner phone.

Installs like an app

Add it to your Windows desktop, taskbar or phone home screen and open it in one tap.

Five reminders. Zero chasing.

Every day at 9:00 AM IST, Corely checks who is coming due and messages them at exactly the right moment. Anyone marked Paid is skipped automatically, so nobody gets a wrong overdue notice.
